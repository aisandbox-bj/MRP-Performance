Prime directive — never hide issues

Impossible values (negative rebuilt stock, dates out of sequence, POs received without a PR) are listed and drill-able — never smoothed out of a chart. Distributions, not averages: still-open items are shown as lower bounds, both tails reachable.

Rollback rule

Before every push: a backup/pre-<chunk> branch and checkpoint/pre-<chunk> tag at the current origin/main, plus a local _rollback/<CHUNK>-pre/ snapshot. Steps are in the Record of Change.

What's shipped v0.1.0-dev

PERF-INT
Intake — supply-chain performance run
Tune's intake, adapted: MB51 + Inventory Master + PR History required; the files decide the scope; PR-History DQ checks (need-by %, PO→receipt join); Delivery Date (yyyymmdd) now read; Tune downloads load too.
PERF-ENGINE
Population engine
Every PR line followed PR → release → PO → 3PL → site → first use with signed durations; daily stock rebuilt for every material. Ties exactly to Trace's chains and Tune's back-calc (built-in parity check).
PERF-TRIGGER
Trigger health & MRP response
Each time stock crosses Min / SS: was anything on order, how long to a PR, a PO and recovery; days exposed with nothing on order; trigger debt vs MRP cadence; quiet stretches.
PERF-LEGS
Process legs as distributions
Approval, buyer, PR → PO, supplier (manufacturer), 3PL, end to end, against plan — histograms with still-open items, out-of-sequence bin, per-month boxes with % closed, breakdowns, drill to both tails.
PERF-STOCK
Order sizing & over / under
V1 order-to-Max ratio at every PR, stock after receipt vs Max, reorder frequency vs plan, receipts vs trigger line, $ over Max, shelf time, stockouts in flight vs nothing on order.
PERF-SEG
Material segments
Drop-in tiles: quartiles (with cut points), top / bottom %, ranges, value picks with include / exclude, text, PR trigger, plus a time window. Every view follows.
PERF-OPS
Change management
Own repo + storage namespace, provenance manifest for borrowed files, Record of Change with rollback, genesis + pre-push checkpoints.
Queue · drag tiles to reorder (saved in this browser only)

Decisions & open questions

Separate app
Own repo (aisandbox-bj/MRP-Performance), own storage namespace (mrpPerf). Tune and Trace are not touched. Tune's shared modules are copied with a provenance manifest (source commit + SHA-256) so drift shows.
Same intake
Tune's intake module, adapted. The input files decide the scope. V1 and PD both assessed. MB51 expected at 150k–500k lines.
Cohort anchoring
Each leg anchored on its own start: PR→PO by PR-created month · supplier by PO month · 3PL by month goods reached the 3PL. Months are provisional until 90% have closed; open items count as lower bounds.
Trigger health
Below the trigger line with NO open PO and NOTHING at the 3PL. A PR alone is not cover. Stock is rebuilt daily from MB51 — no daily snapshots needed.
Vendor, PO data, reporting
Manufacturer stands in for vendor for now. No PO details (next phase, if ever). Workbench first; reporting module later. KPI targets entered once the baseline is seen.
Open — 107 semantics
See the blocked tile on "What's next".
Found while building — PD without safety stock
Zero stock isn't a trigger for PD without SS (MRP acts on reservations). Those stockouts are counted but kept out of trigger-response and trigger-debt, and named on the view.
